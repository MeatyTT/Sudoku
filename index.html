Scan a photo

Take or upload a photo of a printed puzzle. The reader runs in your browser but needs an internet connection the first time, to download itself — then you align a square over the grid and it fills in the numbers for you to double-check.

How to use it

  • type Click a cell and type 1–9. Leave a cell empty for a blank.
  • ⌫ Backspace or 0 clears a cell.
  • ↑↓←→ Arrow keys move between cells.
  • Duplicate numbers in a row, column, or 3×3 box are outlined as you type.

Drag the square over the outer edge of the grid, then confirm.
